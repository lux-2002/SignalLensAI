
# SignalLens AI

## Tagline
Prevent communication breakdown before business damage happens.

## What It Does
SignalLens AI analyzes professional communication and predicts hidden business risks before they escalate into delays, conflicts, or scope creep.

The system detects:
- Hidden expectation mismatch
- Emotional friction
- Scope creep
- Timeline ambiguity
- Ownership confusion
- Communication breakdown risks

It also predicts:
- Potential business impact
- Delivery risk
- Stakeholder dissatisfaction
- Escalation probability
